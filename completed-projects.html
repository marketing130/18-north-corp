Investment Overview

18 North assembled a 283-unit multifamily portfolio across 14 properties through a series of off-market acquisitions in Western New York. The portfolio included a mix of one-, two-, and three-bedroom units across garden-style, townhome, suburban, and urban settings.

Investment Thesis

18 North identified an opportunity to aggregate a fragmented portfolio of well-located multifamily assets at an attractive basis, creating scale across a series of individually sourced acquisitions.

Execution

Through targeted capital investment and active oversight from our in-house asset management team, the portfolio was repositioned through property improvements, operational enhancements, and disciplined management.

Realization

Following execution of the value-add strategy, the portfolio was sold in 2024 for $19.5 million, generating a 33.6% gross levered LP IRR over an average hold period of approximately 34 months.*

Disclosures

* Gross Levered LP IRR reflects aggregate realized performance across the Western New York Portfolio. Average hold period reflects the weighted average investment period across the portfolio.
