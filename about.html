About 18 North

18 North is a multifamily real estate investment, development, and asset management platform focused on acquiring, developing, and operating high-quality communities in select high-growth U.S. markets.

Our business combines disciplined investment underwriting, active asset management, and development expertise to create value throughout the investment lifecycle. By bringing these capabilities together under one platform, we maintain greater alignment, accountability, and execution at every stage of an investment.

Our Approach

Drawing on more than 40 years of combined real estate experience across institutional investment firms, entrepreneurial ventures, and principal ownership, we built 18 North around a simple principle: successful real estate investing requires more than identifying opportunities—it requires the ability to execute.

By combining institutional discipline with entrepreneurial agility across investment, development, construction oversight, and asset management, we maintain greater control over the decisions that shape each investment. This integrated approach enables faster decision-making, stronger accountability, and disciplined execution from acquisition through stabilization and disposition, maximizing value throughout the investment lifecycle for our investors and partners.

Where We Invest

Oklahoma & Northwest Arkansas

18 North focuses on select high-growth markets where demographic trends, employment growth, and housing demand support compelling multifamily investment opportunities. Rather than pursuing broad geographic expansion, we concentrate on markets where local relationships, operational expertise, and disciplined execution create a meaningful competitive advantage.

Our current focus includes Oklahoma and Northwest Arkansas, with the flexibility to pursue opportunities in other markets that align with our investment strategy. By remaining selective, we allocate capital where we believe we can execute with confidence and maximize value throughout the investment lifecycle.
