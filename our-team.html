Managing Partners

Roman Brailovski

Managing Partner & Co-Founder

Roman Brailovski is Co-Founder and Managing Partner of 18 North, where he helps shape the firm's strategic direction and leads investor relations, capital formation, development, construction, and value-add renovation initiatives. Based in the United States, he works closely with the firm's leadership team, investors, and operating partners to support the continued growth of the platform.

With more than 20 years of experience spanning commercial banking, institutional real estate, entrepreneurial investment platforms, and principal ownership, Roman has held senior leadership roles across acquisitions, development, construction, asset management, operations, and capital strategy. His diverse background provides a comprehensive understanding of the investment lifecycle, from capital origination and underwriting through development, operations, and asset realization.

Roman most recently served as Executive Vice President, Operations at CAPREIT, where he was responsible for one of Canada's largest residential real estate portfolios comprising more than 60,000 apartment and manufactured housing sites valued in excess of $16 billion. Earlier in his career, he held leadership positions with Great Gulf Group, Cadillac Fairview, and Bank of Nova Scotia, building expertise across institutional investing, development, capital markets, and large-scale operations.

Roman believes successful real estate investing is built on disciplined execution, aligned partnerships, and direct leadership involvement. Today, he remains closely engaged with investors, partners, and project teams, bringing institutional discipline and the accountability of an owner-operator to every investment undertaken by 18 North.

Ben Friedland

Managing Partner & Co-Founder

Ben Friedland is Co-Founder and Managing Partner of 18 North, where he helps lead the firm's strategic direction and oversees asset management, shared services, business development, and organizational growth. He works closely with the leadership team to strengthen operations, identify investment opportunities, and drive the continued growth of the platform.

With more than 25 years of real estate investment and development experience, Ben has built and led successful real estate platforms across Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom. His expertise spans acquisitions, development, asset management, organizational leadership, and capital deployment, with a primary focus on multifamily investment and long-term value creation.

Throughout his career, Ben has held senior leadership positions across entrepreneurial real estate platforms, including serving as President of Gold Wynn, where he led the growth of a $1.1 billion family office real estate platform with investments across North America and the United Kingdom. He has played a leading role in assembling a 6,000-unit multifamily portfolio valued at more than $750 million and has developed deep expertise in Oklahoma's value-add multifamily sector through decades of sourcing, acquiring, repositioning, and operating apartment communities.

Ben's long-standing market relationships, entrepreneurial leadership, and disciplined approach to asset management continue to shape 18 North's investment strategy and create lasting value for investors and partners.

Senior Leadership

Josh Wynn

Senior Partner, Head of Investments & Capital Markets

As Head of Investments & Capital Markets, Josh Wynn leads 18 North's investment analysis, underwriting, debt financing, and capital markets activities. Working closely with the managing partners, he plays a central role in evaluating acquisitions, structuring financing, and supporting disciplined investment decisions across the firm's portfolio.

With more than 15 years of real estate investment experience, including over a decade focused on Oklahoma's value-add multifamily market, Josh brings deep local market knowledge and expertise spanning acquisitions, underwriting, finance, development, and asset management. Prior to joining 18 North, he served as Director of Finance at Gold Wynn, where he oversaw more than $250 million of acquisitions, dispositions, and financing transactions within a real estate private equity platform managing over $1.5 billion of assets. His combination of local market insight, financial expertise, and disciplined underwriting continues to strengthen 18 North's investment platform.

Shira Brym

Partner, Head of 18 North Asset Management

Shira Brym leads 18 North Asset Management, overseeing portfolio performance, operational strategy, property management, and asset execution across the firm's multifamily investments. Working closely with the managing partners and on-site teams, she is responsible for driving operational excellence and maximizing asset value throughout the investment lifecycle.

With more than a decade of experience spanning institutional real estate asset management, corporate law, and risk management, Shira brings a unique combination of operational and legal expertise to the platform. Prior to joining 18 North, she served in senior asset management and in-house legal roles with a multinational real estate investment and management company overseeing a portfolio exceeding $1 billion across Canada, the United States, and the United Kingdom. In addition to leading the firm's asset management platform, Shira provides strategic in-house legal counsel, supporting governance, risk management, and complex real estate transactions.
