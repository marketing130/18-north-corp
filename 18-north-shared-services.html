Overview

18 North Shared Services is the centralized operating platform that enables every part of our business to perform at its highest level. By integrating finance, technology, marketing, human resources, administration, and corporate operations under one team, we create consistency, efficiency, and scalability across the organization.

Rather than duplicating functions across individual business lines, Shared Services provides institutional-quality infrastructure that allows our investment, development, and asset management teams to focus on creating value.

Our Impact

Operational excellence at scale

By centralizing expertise and resources, Shared Services creates consistency across the 18 North platform. Standardized processes, integrated systems, and dedicated support allow each business line to operate more efficiently while remaining focused on its core responsibilities.

This shared infrastructure reduces duplication, strengthens coordination, and allows the broader platform to scale without sacrificing execution.

Capabilities & Focus

Infrastructure that supports the entire platform

Centralized expertise and resources that enable our teams to operate efficiently, consistently, and at scale.

01 Finance & Accounting

Centralized accounting, financial reporting, treasury, budgeting, and entity-level financial administration.

02 Marketing & Communications

Brand strategy, property marketing, leasing campaigns, investor communications, and digital presence.

03 Technology & Systems

04 Human Resources

05 Corporate & Administrative Operations

Centralized support for vendor management, insurance, corporate administration, office operations, and other shared operational needs.
